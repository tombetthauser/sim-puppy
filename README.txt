SIM PUPPY
A tool for simulating the needs of an Australian Cattle Dog puppy for Danny.

Open index.html in your browser. Keep the images folder next to it.
Photos switch automatically as the dog changes activity or age. The images are
representative: some are reused across activities or ages and may not depict
the exact scene or age in the filename.

Photo sources, authors, licenses, and the filenames using each photo are in
PHOTO-CREDITS.txt. To use a different photo, replace a JPEG in images/ with a
JPEG using exactly the same filename and respect the replacement's license.
The simulator and its local photos do not require an internet connection.

During a care action, the circular countdown shows simulated time remaining.
Practice mode advances simulated time faster; pausing stops the countdown.

ALERT SOUNDS
When a care task is waiting, a distinctive sound (different per task) repeats until you start the matching care action. Alerts get faster and louder the longer the dog waits (full urgency after ~10 min). They pause while an activity is in progress or the game is paused. Use the sound button to enable/mute.
