How to use this trial safely

In real time, actually step away from your normal activity for the suggested duration. A parent handles nighttime care, walks and all child–dog interactions. Never punish accidents or nipping. Reward calm behavior, redirect to toys and use a pen or gate for separation. Crates are for short, comfortable rest, not punishment or all-day confinement.

Young puppies need frequent toilet breaks, lots of sleep, short training and safe socialization. Avoid forced running, long hikes and repetitive jumping until mature and cleared by a vet. In hot weather choose cool hours and indoor enrichment. Ask a veterinarian about vaccination and safe public outings.
